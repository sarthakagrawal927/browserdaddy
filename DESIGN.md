# BrowserDaddy — Tab Scout

Owner delegated the choice on 2026-09-20 after seeing three complete systems.
Tab Scout was selected: a browser-tab-carrying guide, not a storage-box mascot.

## Identity

Use `BrowserDaddyIcon.png` for compact identity: app icon, sidebar and website
mark. Use `BrowserDaddyScout.png` for larger onboarding, About, empty-state and
landing illustrations. Preserve transparency, aspect ratio and clear space.
Never reuse StorageDaddy's box or a cropped illustration contact sheet.

## Native system

Keep the established black surfaces, forest/mint family, cream artwork highlights,
system typography and readable data hierarchy. Existing controls, navigation,
focus, error, disabled and tooltip behavior remain unchanged. Branding is a
precise accent, not a reason to crowd operational tables or add idle animation.
Large art must not be reduced to the compact logo's job.

## Landing

Preserve ios-landings' approved Editorial split layout, responsive image sizing,
semantic controls and reduced-motion support. Use the compact icon in navigation
and the scout as hero artwork. Do not display generated UI mockups as real product
screenshots, personal browsing data, or false native-release claims.

## Product truth

BrowserDaddy combines a local unified history archive with measured foreground
attention. Current native distribution remains a development preview. Classifier
policy and native release qualification are separate open work, not solved by art.
