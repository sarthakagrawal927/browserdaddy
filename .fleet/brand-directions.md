# BrowserDaddy brand direction study — awaiting owner selection

Generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-20. The owner-owned
StorageDaddy mascot was a family reference, not a replacement target.

No current app asset or live landing has been replaced.

## Shared product truth

BrowserDaddy helps Mac users understand and revisit browsing through a unified
history archive and measured frontmost-app attention. Native UI remains SwiftUI.
All artwork must exclude personal browsing data. Landing status stays development
preview, with no invented native download, customer proof or privacy guarantees.
Existing classifier policy requires an owner decision before release.

Keep the recognizable Daddy family: forest-green outlines, mint, cream, rounded
character construction and restrained operational controls. The current storage-box
art is an anti-reference for BrowserDaddy's own subject matter. No external brand
was used or copied. System type is appropriate for native controls; display lettering
in the board is only a visual proposal, not a licensed-font recommendation.

## A — Tab Scout (recommended)

- Thesis: a friendly guide through the browsing day, carrying browser tabs rather
  than a storage box. Tab-shaped app icon and dotted trail provide an ownable motif.
- Layout: compact sidebar and chronological attention ribbon; aligned site rows;
  editorial split hero with an illustrated browsing trail.
- Type/color: rounded wordmark, system UI type, forest structure, mint actions,
  cream character face. Preserve dark native family styling during implementation;
  the generated light content panel is a concept, not approved theme replacement.
- Interaction: selecting a timeline segment reveals its existing details; clear
  hover/focus states, optional brief trail motion respecting reduced motion.
- Risk: mascot must remain secondary to data in the daily-use app.

## B — The Archivist

- Thesis: a bookmark-shaped keeper makes the enduring browsing archive memorable.
- Layout: library-ledger hierarchy, search first, month rail, dated rows; quieter
  editorial landing with a fan of tab-shaped cards.
- Type/color: small-serif display proposal, compact system rows, paper/forest
  surfaces, mint index markers. Light-native treatment needs explicit approval.
- Interaction: date/source filtering and row inspection, not decorative card stacks
  inside operational tables.
- Risk: visually emphasizes history more than the attention feature.

## C — Focus Lens

- Thesis: a single lens in a browser-window character makes measured attention the
  product's distinguishing signal.
- Layout: strong current-app header, broad readable attention timeline, disciplined
  inspector; oversized sculptural lens in the split landing hero.
- Type/color: restrained squared wordmark, prominent readable numerals, charcoal,
  cream and minted-green planes; no glow or gratuitous glass effects.
- Interaction: inspect recorded intervals with precise states; no invented live
  navigation graph or website-content surveillance.
- Risk: lens imagery can suggest surveillance; copy must stress user-controlled
  collection and explain actual permission/privacy boundaries.
